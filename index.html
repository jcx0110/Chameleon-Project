Motivation

The clue disappears. The decision remains.

In long-horizon manipulation, the information that determines an action may appear long before the robot needs to act. At the decision point, the same visible scene can require different actions depending on its history. We call this gap observation–action delay.

Dataset + demos

Camo-Dataset

A real-robot benchmark designed to isolate memory-dependent decisions from manipulation execution: three tasks, 120 teleoperated demonstrations per task, two RGB views, and deliberately ambiguous decision scenes.

Evaluation + results

Memory changes what the robot can do.

Camo-Dataset reports manipulation success (MSR), history-correct decision success (DSR), and end-to-end success (SR) separately. Chameleon improves the decision—not only the motion.
